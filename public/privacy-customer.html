Executive Statement: This Privacy Policy details how Local Bandi collects, safeguards, and responsibly utilizes customer information when browsing local stores, ordering groceries/food, scheduling appointments, or booking home services.

1. Introduction & Applicability

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Local Bandi - Customer App (Package ID: com.localbandi.customer) operated by Local Bandi Multi Services Private Limited. It governs how we collect, store, safeguard, and process personal and operational telemetry data in full compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, the Information Technology Act, 2000 (India), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).

4. Data Storage, Retention & Anonymization

We maintain strict retention limitations and military-grade cloud encryption:

  • Storage Infrastructure: Google Cloud Firestore (Mumbai Region)
  • Encryption at Rest: AES-256 encryption on all stored database records
  • Encryption in Transit: HTTPS / TLS 1.3 cryptographic transport
  • Data Retention Policy: Order records are retained as required by Indian consumer protection and GST regulations. Active customer cart and session caches are refreshed automatically.
  • Data Anonymization: Search queries and analytics logs are aggregated and unlinked from personal identity.

5. Technical Security Safeguards

We implement layered technical and organizational security controls to protect user data against unauthorized access, loss, or manipulation:

  • Zero plain-text password architecture; all authentication is verified via secure OTP and cryptographic tokens.
  • No storage of sensitive financial credentials: Credit/Debit card numbers, CVVs, and UPI PINs are NEVER stored on our servers.
  • Strict data isolation: Customers can never query or view orders belonging to other platform users.
  • Automated vulnerability scanning and continuous firewall protection on all API endpoints.

6. Purpose Limitation & Data Usage Policies

Our commitment to user privacy and ethical data handling:

  • Data is used solely to facilitate consumer purchases, customer support, and delivery tracking.
  • We strictly DO NOT sell or rent your personal information to third-party advertisers.
  • You have total control to update your profile, remove saved addresses, or delete your account at any time.
Zero Data Brokerage Guarantee: Local Bandi does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade personal data, customer contacts, merchant pricing secrets, or delivery partner tracking data with third-party data aggregators or advertising networks under any circumstances.

8. Google Play Mandatory Data & Account Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's 2024+ User Data Policy on Account Deletion, all registered users have the absolute right to request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data.

In-App Deletion Procedure:

  1. Open Customer App -> Tap on 'Profile' or 'Settings' tab.
  2. Scroll down to 'Privacy & Security'.
  3. Select 'Delete My Account & Personal Data'.
  4. Verify your mobile number via OTP. Your account and active sessions will be terminated immediately.

Direct Email Deletion Request:

Send an email from your registered email ID to tharunbandi614@gmail.com with the subject 'Account Deletion Request - Local Bandi Customer App'.

Scrubbing Timeline: Personal contact details, delivery addresses, and saved preferences are permanently scrubbed within 14 calendar days.

Legal Retention Exceptions: Past invoices, GST tax audit data, and records of fulfilled orders must be retained for the statutory period mandated by law.

9. Children's Privacy
