4. Data Storage, Retention & Anonymization

We maintain strict retention limitations and military-grade cloud encryption:

  • Storage Infrastructure: Google Cloud Firestore & Cloud Storage (India Region)
  • Encryption at Rest: AES-256 database-level encryption
  • Encryption in Transit: TLS 1.3 cryptographic transport
  • Data Retention Policy: Business records, tax invoices, and settlement ledgers are retained for 8 years as required by Indian GST and company compliance laws.
  • Data Anonymization: Customer contact info transmitted for delivery coordination is automatically purged 24 hours post-fulfillment.

5. Technical Security Safeguards

We implement layered technical and organizational security controls to protect user data against unauthorized access, loss, or manipulation:

  • Role-based access control ensuring merchant staff can only view and manage their assigned store branch.
  • Bank account details masked on merchant dashboard to prevent unauthorized internal snooping.
  • Immutable order ledger: once an order is settled, the financial split record cannot be altered retroactively.
  • Two-factor authentication for bank account updates and critical payout credential changes.

6. Purpose Limitation & Data Usage Policies

Our commitment to user privacy and ethical data handling:

  • Business and menu catalog data is published publicly to consumers to drive customer discovery and sales.
  • Financial data is used strictly for bank settlement and tax withholding.
  • We do not disclose vendor wholesale purchasing secrets or proprietary operational metrics.
Zero Data Brokerage Guarantee: Local Bandi does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade personal data, customer contacts, merchant pricing secrets, or delivery partner tracking data with third-party data aggregators or advertising networks under any circumstances.

8. Google Play Mandatory Data & Account Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's 2024+ User Data Policy on Account Deletion, all registered users have the absolute right to request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data.

In-App Deletion Procedure:

  1. Open Vendor App -> Go to Store Settings.
  2. Scroll to 'Merchant Agreement & Account Status'.
  3. Click 'Request Store Offboarding & Deletion'.
  4. Settle all pending orders and COD balances with the platform.

Direct Email Deletion Request:

Submit a formal store closure request to tharunbandi111@gmail.com with your Store ID, Registered Phone Number, and Trade License.

Scrubbing Timeline: Storefront listing is removed within 24 hours; non-statutory data is scrubbed within 30 days of final ledger settlement.

Legal Retention Exceptions: GST invoices, completed order receipts, and tax records are retained in compliance with statutory Indian accounting obligations.
