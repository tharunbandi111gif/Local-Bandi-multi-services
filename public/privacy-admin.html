4. Data Storage, Retention & Anonymization

We maintain strict retention limitations and military-grade cloud encryption:

  • Storage Infrastructure: Google Cloud Platform (Secure Multi-Region Mirroring with Immutable Backups)
  • Encryption at Rest: FIPS 140-2 Level 3 validated hardware-backed encryption
  • Encryption in Transit: Strict TLS 1.3 with Certificate Pinning
  • Data Retention Policy: Administrative audit trails and ledger changes are immutable and preserved for 10 years to comply with financial regulatory mandates.
  • Data Anonymization: Access to raw personal data is strictly audited and subject to automated obfuscation.

5. Technical Security Safeguards

We implement layered technical and organizational security controls to protect user data against unauthorized access, loss, or manipulation:

  • Zero-Trust Architecture: Every administrative request is verified for identity, device posture, and authorization.
  • IP-allowlisting and geo-fencing for Master Admin access portals.
  • Automated automated real-time alert triggers upon any unauthorized privilege escalation attempt.
  • Continuous daily automated database snapshots and disaster recovery readiness.

6. Purpose Limitation & Data Usage Policies

Our commitment to user privacy and ethical data handling:

  • Master Administrative access is exercised solely for platform reliability, security enforcement, and legal compliance.
  • Direct modification of database records without an audit trail is technically blocked by system architecture.
  • All platform governance actions are recorded permanently in tamper-evident logs.
Zero Data Brokerage Guarantee: Local Bandi does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade personal data, customer contacts, merchant pricing secrets, or delivery partner tracking data with third-party data aggregators or advertising networks under any circumstances.
