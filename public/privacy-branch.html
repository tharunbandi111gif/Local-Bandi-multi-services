1. Introduction & Applicability

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Local Bandi - Branch Admin App (Package ID: com.localbandi.branch) operated by Local Bandi Multi Services Private Limited. It governs how we collect, store, safeguard, and process personal and operational telemetry data in full compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, the Information Technology Act, 2000 (India), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA).

4. Data Storage, Retention & Anonymization

We maintain strict retention limitations and military-grade cloud encryption:

  • Storage Infrastructure: Google Cloud Firestore (Encrypted Multi-Tenant Partitioning)
  • Encryption at Rest: AES-256 military-grade encryption
  • Encryption in Transit: TLS 1.3 / HTTPS with HSTS enforced
  • Data Retention Policy: Operational logs are preserved for the duration of the active franchise agreement plus 5 years for legal compliance.
  • Data Anonymization: End-customer identifiers are masked from branch staff view unless needed for an active escalatory dispute.

5. Technical Security Safeguards

We implement layered technical and organizational security controls to protect user data against unauthorized access, loss, or manipulation:

  • Strict territory partitioning: Branch admins cannot access data, merchants, or orders outside their designated geofence.
  • Hardware session binding and IP auditing to prevent unauthorized remote account sharing.
  • Detailed audit logging for every merchant approval, driver activation, or commission query.
  • Role-based privilege restrictions preventing alteration of master platform parameters.

6. Purpose Limitation & Data Usage Policies

Our commitment to user privacy and ethical data handling:

  • Regional data is utilized solely for supervising local fulfillment, supporting partners, and tracking franchise earnings.
  • Confidential merchant and customer data must never be exported or used for unauthorized commercial ventures.
  • Franchise data access is revoked immediately upon termination of the franchise agreement.
Zero Data Brokerage Guarantee: Local Bandi does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade personal data, customer contacts, merchant pricing secrets, or delivery partner tracking data with third-party data aggregators or advertising networks under any circumstances.
